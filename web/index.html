Is a financial crisis building?

CrisisPredictor reads fourteen live signals from credit markets, stock markets, the economy and the news. It scores each one against historical thresholds and combines them into a single 0 to 100 Crisis Score.

From raw data to one number

Fourteen signals go in and one score comes out. Every step is written down and can be checked.

  1. It reads live data

    Yield curve, credit spreads, the VIX, drawdown, jobless claims, CPI, oil, gold and the dollar come from FRED and Yahoo Finance. Fresh news on tariffs and conflict comes from a live search.

  2. An AI scores each signal against history

    For every signal the model retrieves the matching section of a reference document with thresholds. It scores the signal from -10 to +10 against those thresholds only, never against its own opinion.

  3. Plain code sets the weights

    The scores are combined with fixed weights written in Python. The AI never decides the final number.

  4. You get a score, a level and an alarm

    The result is a Crisis Score from 0 to 100, a level from Calm to Crisis, and an alarm that switches on when the score reaches the Stress line.
